OUR PURPOSE

Analysis

Mengenal permasalahan dan solusi dari limbah DVD.

Permasalahan

DVD yang sudah tidak digunakan sering kali menumpuk dan menjadi limbah elektronik. Jika tidak dikelola dengan baik, limbah tersebut dapat mencemari lingkungan.

Solusi

Melalui kegiatan daur ulang, DVD bekas dapat dimanfaatkan menjadi berbagai barang kreatif yang memiliki fungsi dan nilai ekonomi.

ASSYIFA FITRI FATEHA

Penyelesaian Soal SPLDV

1. Permisalan

Misalkan:

x = Harga 1 Pot Keyboard
y = Harga 1 LumiCLock

2. Model Matematika

Paket 1:

4 pot keyboard dan 9 LumiClock seharga 370.000

Paket 2:

5 pot keyboard dan 4 LumiClock seharga 245.000

3. Membuat Persamaan

4x + 9y = 370.000...(1)
5x + 4y = 245.000...(2)

4. Penyelesaian SPLDV

A. Metode Eliminasi

4x + 9y = 370.000 |× 4
5x + 4y = 245.000 |× 9

16x + 36y = 1.480.000
45x + 36y = 2.205.000
_____________________ −
-29x = -725.000
x = -725.000 / -29
x = 25.000

B. Metode Substitusi

Masukkan x = 10.000 ke persamaan (1).

4x + 9y = 370.000
4(25.000) + 9y = 370.000
100.000 + 9y = 370.000
9y = 370.000 − 100.000
9y = 270.000
9y = 270.000 / 9
y = 30.000

5. Hasil Akhir

  • x= 25.000
  • y= 30.000

6. Kesimpulan

Jadi harga satuan Pot Keyboard adalah Rp25.000 dan harga satuan LumiClock adalah Rp30.000

Jawaban LKPD Matematika SPLDV

Dyah ayu fitrianingsih

Penyelesaian Soal SPLDV

1. Menentukan Variabel

Misalkan:
x = Harga satu EcoClock
y = Harga satu keylight

2. Membuat Model Matematika

Paket 1:

2 ecoclock dan 1 keylight seharga Rp90.000

Paket 2:

3 ecoclock dan 5 keylight seharga 275.000

3. Membuat Persamaan

2x + y = 90.000 ... (1)
3x + 5y = 275.000 ... (2)

4. Penyelesaian Metode Campuran

A. Metode Eliminasi

Persamaan (1) dikalikan 5:
2x + y = 90.000 |x5

Persamaan (2) dikalikan 1:
3x + 5y = 275.000 |x1

10x + 5y = 450.000
3x + 5y = 275.000
____________________ -
7x = 175.000
x = 175.000 : 7
x = 25.000

B. Metode Substitusi

Masukkan x = 25.000 ke persamaan (1).

2x + y = 90.000
2(25.000) + y = 90.000
50.000 + y = 90.000
y = 90.000 - 50.000
y = 40.000

5. Hasil Akhir

Jadi, hasilnya adalah:

  • Harga 1 Ecoclock = Rp25.000
  • Harga 1 keylight = Rp40.000

6. Kesimpulan

Jadi, harga satu EcoClock adalah Rp25.000 dan harga satu KeyLight adalah Rp40.000.

Jawaban LKPD Matematika SPLDV

FENTI ZULFATUL ARIFAH

Penyelesaian Soal SPLDV

1. Menentukan Variabel

Misalkan:
x = Harga 1 Keylight
y = Harga 1 Reglowtree

2. Membuat Model Matematika

Paket 1:

5 Keylight + 7 Reglowtree = Rp215.000.

Paket 2:

3 Keylight + 2 Reglowtree = Rp85.000

3. Membuat Persamaan

5x + 7y = 215.000 ...(1)
3x + 2y = 85.000 ...(2)

4. Penyelesaian Metode Campuran

A. Metode Eliminasi

Persamaan (1) dikalikan 2:
5x + 7y = 215.000 | x 2

Persamaan (2) dikalikan 7:
3x + 2y = 85.000 | × 7

10x + 14y = 430.000
21x + 14y = 595.000
_____________________-
-11x = -165.000
x = 165.000 : 11
x = 15.000

B. Metode Substitusi

Masukkan x = 15.000 ke persamaan (2).

3x + 2y = 85.000
3(15.000) + 2y = 85.000
45.000 + 2y = 85.000
2y = 85.000 - 45.000
2y = 40.000
y = 40.000 : 2
y = 20.000

5. Hasil Akhir

Jadi, hasilnya adalah:

  • Harga 1 Keylight = Rp15.000
  • Harga 1 Reglowtree = Rp20.000

6. Kesimpulan

Jadi, harga satu produk Keylight adalah Rp15.000 dan harga satu produk Reglowtree adalah Rp20.000. Hasil tersebut sesuai dengan harga kedua paket pada gambar.

How Unused DVDs Are Transformed into a Decorative Lamp

  In the past, DVDs were used to store movies, music, and various memories. Today, many of them are forgotten and left unused because of modern digital technology. Instead of allowing these shiny discs to become useless waste, they can be given a second life through creativity. One unique idea is transforming old DVDs into a decorative lamp, turning forgotten technology into a source of light and beauty.

  The process begins by collecting several unused DVDs and giving them a new purpose. Each disc is cleaned and prepared before being arranged into a vertical structure. A gallon cap is used as the foundation, while a USB LED strip is prepared to create the lighting effect. The DVDs, which once carried digital information, are now ready to become part of something completely different.

  The DVDs are carefully stacked horizontally to form a tower-like shape with reflective surfaces. They are connected using hot glue to maintain the structure. A long LED strip is placed between the layers, allowing the light to shine through the gaps and reflect across the shiny DVD surfaces. Slowly, ordinary discarded discs turn into a unique decorative object with an interesting visual effect.

  As a result, unused DVDs are transformed into a decorative lamp that has both functional and aesthetic value. The product can be used as a room decoration while providing additional lighting. This transformation reduces electronic waste and encourages the reuse of available materials. Therefore, recycling unused DVDs into decorative products is one way to support a greener future.

Decorative Lamp from Unused DVDs

  A decorative lamp from unused DVDs is an innovative and eco-friendly solution for managing electronic waste. This simple craft turns old DVDs into a beautiful lamp, making it an attractive decoration while helping to reduce waste. However, many people still throw away DVDs without realizing that they can be turned into something useful and creative.

  The transformation begins with cleaning the unused DVDs and cutting them into flower petal shapes, such as for the lotus flower. The pieces are then arranged on a lamp frame and attached using glue. Finally, a light bulb is placed inside, allowing the unused DVD pieces to reflect beautiful colors when the lamp is turned on. Instead of throwing away the DVD, it becomes a stunning and functional decorative lamp.

  This decorative lamp is not only beautiful, but also has many benefits. It helps reduce electronic waste, is easy to make with simple materials, and can be used as a unique decoration in bedrooms, study rooms, or living rooms. With a little creativity, unused DVDs can be turned into a useful product that is both aesthetic and environmentally friendly.

  As a result, the decorative lamp from unused DVDs is both functional and environmentally friendly. It not only reduces waste but also inspires people to be more creative in reusing unused items. The lamp can be a simple yet meaningful step toward a cleaner planet and a more sustainable environment.

How Ecolotus Lamp Is Made from Unused CDs

Ecolotus Lamp

  Electronic waste is one of the environmental problems caused by the increasing use of technology. Unused CDs are often thrown away because they are no longer needed. However, these CDs can still be reused to create beautiful and useful products. One creative way to recycle unused CDs is by transforming them into a decorative flower lamp called Ecolotus Lamp. This product shows how waste materials can become an attractive and valuable decoration.

  The process begins by collecting several unused CDs and preparing other materials, such as colored paper, LED lights, pearls, and glue. First, the CDs are cleaned and arranged into layers to form the base of the lamp. Next, colored paper is cut and shaped into pink flower petals and green leaves. The petals are then arranged carefully to create a beautiful lotus flower.

  After the lotus flower is formed, it is attached to the top of the CD base. Small pearls are added as decorations to make the product look more attractive. Then, LED lights are placed around the CD base to create a glowing effect. The LED lights are connected to electrical wires and plugged into a power outlet. All the parts are carefully attached and arranged until they form a complete decorative flower lamp called Ecolotus Lamp.

  As a result, unused CDs are transformed into Ecolotus Lamp, a beautiful decorative lamp inspired by a lotus flower. The lamp can be used as a room decoration and creates a warm atmosphere when the lights are turned on. This creative project helps reduce electronic waste and encourages people to reuse materials instead of throwing them away.
